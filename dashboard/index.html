      How a field is judged

      A regrowth peak counts only if it stands 0.20 NDVI above the low points on both sides. The field's cycle is the median spacing between its peaks, bounded to 18–60 days.

      Expected NDVI at day d of the cycle is the median of what this same field read d ± 3 days after each of its previous cuts.

      priority = deficit × hectares, and exactly zero when a field is on schedule — so a freshly cut field can never enter the queue.

      Data and screening

      —

      Cloud, shadow and snow removed via the scene classification layer (classes 0, 1, 3, 8, 9, 10, 11). A field-date is kept only when more than 60% of that field's pixels survive screening.

      Hyperspectral

      EMIT narrow-band indices are shown per field where a scene covers it, as measurement. They rank nothing.

      On this dataset they improved a random-fold comparison by +0.12 AUC but made prediction worse on a held-out scene. Two scenes cannot separate physiology from acquisition conditions.
