A February acquisition does not look like a July one. With two scenes, random folds cannot separate plant physiology from atmospheric conditions.

Adding the narrow bands makes prediction worse on a scene the model has not seen — in one case worse than a coin flip.

2 of 60 EMIT granules over this area matched a Sentinel-2 day within two days. Leave-one-scene-out needs six to eight.

This is the slide that should win technical credibility. We found the artefact in our own headline result before shipping it. The check took four lines of code and is the difference between a finding and an artefact. Say that we report the narrow bands as measurement and rank nothing with them.
