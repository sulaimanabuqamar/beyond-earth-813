Honest limits, and what comes next

What this is not, and what we would do about it

Not yet validated

No field visit, no irrigation log, no yield record. Every status is a screening result. The tool says “look at this field first”, never “this field is water-stressed”.

Needs two cuts

A field with fewer than two clean peaks gets the regional 30-day default and is flagged as such. Newly planted fields are the weak case.

Relative, not absolute

A field declining across several cycles is judged against its own already-degraded history, and will under-flag.

If we go to incubation

1 · Ground truth. One operator, one season of irrigation records against our queue. Turns a screening tool into a measured one and sets the price.

2 · Regional scale-up. The full Wadi as-Sirhan belt and one UAE region. A compute cost, not a research cost.

3 · Deploy the API. Auth, rate limits, published spec, one reference integration.

4 · More scenes. Six to eight matched EMIT acquisitions would settle the question we left open. Upside, not dependency.

Every limitation above is in the methodology report, with the measurement that establishes it.

Do not soften these. The limitation slide is where a technical jury decides whether to trust the rest of the deck. The strongest thing we can say is that we know exactly what we have not proven.
