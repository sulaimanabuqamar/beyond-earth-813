Detection is a shape test

NDVI above 0.30, then keep only what is shaped like a pivot: 10–400 ha, eccentricity at most 0.75, solidity at least 0.85. No boundaries were supplied and none were drawn by hand.

Detect at peak green

The date is chosen automatically: the greenest fully covered day of the season. Here, 13 April — p95 NDVI 0.887, 100% clear. Eight of fourteen probed dates were discarded for partial coverage. On an earlier run, detecting in April found 511 fields and detecting in July only 328 — the same confound, attacking the step that builds the field set.

Sentinel-2 L2A via Microsoft Planetary Computer, cloud < 20%, mosaicked by solar day, SCL classes 0/1/3/8/9/10/11 removed. 20 m for detection, 60 m for the series.

The 511-versus-328 difference is worth pausing on: it is the same confound appearing in the detection step, and it is why the detection date is chosen by the algorithm rather than by us. The cycle length is measured, not assumed — a median of 30.5 days across 493 of the 518 fields; the other 25 never showed a clean peak and fall back to a 30-day default.
