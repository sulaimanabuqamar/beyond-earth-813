The confound

Vegetation here is a sawtooth, not a trend

Every cut drops a field from about 0.90 NDVI to about 0.35 within ten days. Then it regrows, and thirty days later it is cut again.

On the dashed line both fields read the same. Only what happens afterwards tells them apart.

Blue — cut, regrows on schedule

Red — stressed, never comes back

Schematic of the mechanism. NDVI levels and the ~30-day cadence are taken from the measured series.

Say plainly this chart is a schematic, not measured data — the measured version is the next slide. The point to land: rank by decline and you are ranking by how recently the mower came through.
