The hyperspectral question

Our own best idea, set up as a fair test

Sentinel-2 has no band at 531, 570 or 970 nm. NASA's EMIT has 285 of them. The claim was that narrow bands could separate a cut field from a stressed one where NDVI cannot. We tested it as a prediction contest: labels taken from what each field did 15–45 days afterwards, rows restricted to fields already low on the day, folds split by field, twenty repeats.

Better in 20 of 20 repeats, and it survives removing EMIT's own NDVI — so it is not merely a second NDVI measurement. On this evidence the differentiator is proven.

286 field-observations · 22 recover, 264 stay low · cross-validated AUC, mean of 20 repeats · from the run with NASA Earthdata credentials supplied
