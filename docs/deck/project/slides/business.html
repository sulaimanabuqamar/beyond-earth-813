The case

Cost scales with area, not with customers

Who buys

Pivot operators — scouting time is the scarce resource.
Water regulators — allocation needs per-field evidence.
Lenders and insurers — crop condition without site visits.
Farm software — they own the customer, we own the cycle model.

Priced per hectare per season

We have deliberately not set the rate. It depends on what one avoided inspection is worth to a real operator, and that is the first question a pilot answers.

The unit economics

One area-season — 518 fields, 82 dates, 33,078 field-days — runs end to end on a free-tier notebook, on imagery that costs nothing and needs no credentials.

The marginal cost of adding a field is effectively zero.

What is defensible

Not the maths — anyone can compute NDVI. It is having tested the obvious approach, found it wrong, and published the check that overturned our own headline.

Field counts and dataset size measured from the committed run. Pricing shape is a stated modelling choice, not researched market data.

Be explicit that we did not invent a price. Judges on criterion 7 respond better to a model with named assumptions than to a fabricated TAM. Every figure on this slide is from our own committed run.
